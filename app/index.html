    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4 out of 10 (PowerPyx). No online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission gold medals — and no mission replay, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter. The checklist below is built from the official trophy descriptions.

      Time and difficulty estimates: PowerPyx, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap. The 100% checklist appears only after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, but progress made during the story already counts.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list can be missed. Everything else stays available in free-roam after the story — but read the watch list too, because a few strands are time-sensitive even though they are not missable.

      Save discipline (no mission replay)

      The game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave slot and no mission replay. PowerPyx recommends a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), then rotating the remaining 5 for free-roam clean-up. Keep a Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops.

      Watch list — not missable, but do them on time

      • Race Against Yago (part of Strangers & Freaks): Output Lag flags this Chapter 3 race as time-sensitive — it carries an hourglass icon. Do Yago's strand the moment it appears.
      • Optional missions with an hourglass icon disappear after a while, and some quest givers go away temporarily (for example Kenton after Chapter 1). PowerPyx says this does not block any trophy: trophy-related quest givers always return, and there are far more optional missions than the 100 needed for the checklist.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving (PowerPyx). Keep cheats off on your trophy save.

      Missable assessment: PowerPyx, GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap; time-sensitive race: Output Lag, GTA 6 Trophy List.
