    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, in order. The story cannot be rushed past, the stranger strands open as you explore, and the long counts are best left for last. Built from the official trophy descriptions alone.

      Missable trophies — do these on time

      Most of the 55 can be earned any time after the story. The ones below need a moment that may not come back: a partner beside you, a place you can only enter once, or a choice you only get to make once. Do them the first time the chance appears.
